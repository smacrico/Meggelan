
# Alert Thresholds v1

These thresholds are conservative starter rules and must be personalized after 60–90 days of clean baseline data.

| Signal | Yellow | Red |
|---|---:|---:|
| RMSSD / HRV drop vs baseline | >10% for 2–3 days | >20% for 3+ days |
| Resting HR rise vs baseline | >5% for 2–3 days | >8% for 3+ days |
| Sleep duration drop | >10% | >20% |
| Fatigue increase vs baseline | +2/10 | +3/10 |
| Steps/activity drop | >20% | >35% |
| Symptom burden | average >3/10 | average >5/10 |

## Escalation logic
Red alert is stronger when:
- HRV suppression + RHR elevation + fatigue increase occur together
- symptoms are new/worsening for >24 hours
- no fever, infection, heat exposure, or obvious stressor explains the worsening
